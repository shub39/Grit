Grit combines a to-do list and a daily habit tracker in one focused, ad-free app — so you can plan your day and build routines in the same place.

Whether you want a simple checklist, a habit streak, or a measurable goal, Grit keeps everything on your phone, offline and in sync.

▸ TASKS THAT STAY ORGANIZED
• Categorize tasks into custom lists
• Add subtasks by long-pressing a task
• Set per-task reminders and mark tasks done straight from the notification
• Drag to reorder your list, and reorder automatically as tasks are completed

▸ HABITS THAT ACTUALLY STICK
• Yes/No habits and Measurable habits with a target value and unit (8 km, 3 hours, 10 pages…)
• Choose which days of the week each habit runs on
• Get a daily reminder for any habit
• Current streak, the best streak and consistency score for every habit

▸ SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Habit heat map and calendar view of completed days
• Weekly comparison charts, up to a full year of history
• Week day breakdown to find when you are most consistent
• Monthly and overall analytics across all your habits

▸ BUILT FOR YOUR HOME SCREEN
• Four widgets: All Tasks, Habit Overview, Habit Streak and Habit Week Chart
• Live widget previews and quick refresh

▸ MAKE IT YOURS
• Material 3 Expressive design with Material You wallpaper colours
• AMOLED dark theme, custom fonts and custom colour palettes
• Biometric app lock, 24-hour clock, and configurable week start
• Adapts to tablets, foldables and desktop

▸ YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
• Works fully offline — no account required
• Export and restore your data any time
• Open source, GPL-3.0
• Available in 35+ languages

Grit Plus unlocks extra themes, unlimited habits, subtasks and full analytics. Prefer everything unlocked? The FOSS variant on GitHub and F-Droid includes every feature for free.

Made with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Open source under GPL-3.0 — contributions welcome.
